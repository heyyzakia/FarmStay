About Us

A quiet luxury, held by water

Tucked into the hills of Thandavapura, XYZ Resorts is built around a single idea: that rest should feel like floating. Our villas open onto private decks, our infinity pool blurs into the sky, and every plate on our menu is grown within walking distance of the kitchen. This is a resort for people who travel to slow down.
